Who We Are

Happy Paws is an online pet store. Our goal is to provide healthy, lovable, and affordable pets to families. We believe that every pet deserves a caring home.

What You’ll Find at Happy paws

Pet Care Guides: Easy-to-follow tips for dogs, cats & other furry friends pets illness sign: all the diseases that are faced by animals and there treatement. Training Advice: Step-by-step help to raise well-behaved, happy pets

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect loving owners with healthy pets. We ensure that all pets are vaccinated, well-groomed, and handled with care.

Call to action

Every pet deserves a loving home, and you can make that difference today. Browse our available pets and find your perfect companion. join our community of animal lovers and help us give every pet the care, love, and home they deserve!

The Team

The team behind PAW are seasoned fosterers who saw first hand the burden the rescues were under. We created PAW to help the rescues, but also to help the public find the right animal in order to reduce the demand for puppy farms and breeders.
