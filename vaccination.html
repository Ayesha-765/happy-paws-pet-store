Pet Vaccinations

Protect your furry friends with essential vaccinations 🐶🐱

Why Vaccinations Matter

Vaccinations are one of the most important ways to keep your pet healthy and prevent serious diseases. Just like humans, pets need protection against various illnesses that can be life-threatening.

Common Pet Vaccinations

  • Rabies: Required by law in many areas and protects against a deadly virus.
  • Distemper: A highly contagious disease affecting dogs.
  • Parvovirus: A serious illness that can cause severe diarrhea and dehydration in puppies.
  • Bordetella: Protects against kennel cough.
  • Feline Leukemia: A common and often fatal disease in cats.
  • Feline Panleukopenia: Also known as feline distemper.

Vaccination Schedule

Puppies and kittens require a series of vaccinations starting at 6-8 weeks old, with boosters every 3-4 weeks until 16 weeks. Adult pets need annual or triennial boosters depending on the vaccine and local regulations.
